## 1. What we heard
You run a busy two-dentist NHS and private practice in Harrogate. About 160 calls come in each week and 30–40 go unanswered, mostly first thing and at lunch; after 17:30 it is voicemail, and half of callers don't leave a message. Most calls are routine check-up and hygiene bookings, a steady share ask whether you are taking NHS patients, and about one in ten is an emergency. Your website is not mobile-friendly and has no online booking.

The three problems:
1. Missed calls mean missed bookings and lost new private patients.
2. Reception spends its time on routine booking calls.
3. The website does not let patients book or find answers on a phone.

## 2. What we'll build
**AI receptionist (our AI receptionist service)**
- Answers when reception is busy, at lunch and out of hours, in a natural voice, using the practice name; says it is an AI assistant if asked.
- Answers your common questions, including your current NHS position in your own words, which you can change at any time.
- Takes check-up and hygiene bookings and changes, and sends reception a summary of every call.
- Emergencies go to a member of staff, or are taken as an urgent message, following rules you approve.

**Website (our website service)**
- A modern, mobile-friendly site for the practice, with an online booking button, clear NHS/private information, team details and the information dental practices are expected to show.

**Extra work specific to your practice (priced separately)**
- **Dentally connection:** the receptionist checks free check-up and hygiene slots and books or moves them directly in Dentally. This depends on Dentally giving access; if they don't, the receptionist takes the booking request and reception confirms it in Dentally.
- **Emergency triage:** a short set of questions, written and approved by Dr Hassan, that places emergencies on your emergency slot list and sends anything serious to staff, 999 or NHS 111. The receptionist never gives clinical advice.
- **Optional:** a short training session for your reception team; a follow-up text for missed calls; a weekly calls report.

## 7. Data and security
Calls to a dental practice involve health information, which UK GDPR treats as special category data. We act as your data processor under a data processing agreement, help you complete a data protection impact assessment before any real calls, and keep your data in your own systems (Dentally and your mailbox) and our voice platform only. Call recording is off unless you choose it. Your data is kept separate from every other client and never used to train AI models. Website and receptionist wording about your services is approved by you, so it fits your GDC obligations on advertising.
