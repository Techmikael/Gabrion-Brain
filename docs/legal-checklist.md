# Legal basics checklist (UK)

Not legal advice: confirm with an accountant or solicitor.

- [ ] Company registered at Companies House; business bank account
- [ ] Register with the ICO and pay the data protection fee
- [ ] Privacy notice on gabrion's website
- [ ] Standard client contract + data processing agreement (DPA) under UK GDPR Article 28
- [ ] List of sub-processors (AI model provider, hosting, voice platform, CRM) for the DPA
- [ ] Professional indemnity insurance
- [ ] Cyber insurance
- [ ] Health clients: expect DSPT/NHS data requirements; finance clients: expect FCA-related supplier due diligence
- [ ] PECR rules for marketing email and calls
- [ ] Records of processing activity (simple spreadsheet is fine to start)
