# Workflow: Friday learning review

**Lead:** chief-of-staff · **Ends with:** proposed playbook changes in the approval queue

| # | Step | Who | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Read this week's lines in every `memory/lessons/*.md` | chief-of-staff | List of lessons |
| 2 | Group them: repeated (3+), founder lessons, one-offs | chief-of-staff with `product-feedback-synthesizer` | Groups |
| 3 | Lessons that matter to everyone go into `memory/lessons/shared.md` | chief-of-staff | Shared lessons |
| 4 | Repeated and founder lessons become proposed playbook or workflow changes | chief-of-staff with `specialized-workflow-architect` | Queue item `new-or-unusual` with the exact wording |
| 5 | Agents with many corrections: suggest a coach, a new specialist, or `bench` | chief-of-staff | Note in the Monday briefing |
| 6 | Three lines in the Monday briefing: what the team learned | `support-executive-summary-generator` | Briefing section |
