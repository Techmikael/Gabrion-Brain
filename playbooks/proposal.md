# Playbook: Proposal

**Owner:** solutions-architect · **Client journey:** step 4 · **Approval:** founder approves every proposal and price

## Steps
1. Start from `requirements.md` in the client vault.
2. Map each must-have to a factory in `factories/`. Anything that no factory covers is **custom**: list it separately with its own price and risk.
3. Price from `docs/pricing.md`: setup fee per factory + monthly managed fee + custom items. Never discount; a discount is the founder's decision.
4. Write the proposal in `clients/<slug>/proposal/proposal.md` using the outline below.
5. Add an approval item: type `price`, prepared work = the proposal path, risk = the custom items and any client data involved.
6. When APPROVED: Finance prepares the contract and deposit invoice; Front Desk or the founder sends it.

## Proposal outline (one to two pages)
1. **What we heard** – their situation in their words, the 3 problems.
2. **What we'll build** – per factory, in plain words, what they get.
3. **How it works** – the 8-step journey: build on staging, they review, you approve launch.
4. **Timeline** – weeks, with the review point.
5. **Price** – setup, monthly, what's included, what's not.
6. **Managed or handed over** – both options and the difference.
7. **Data and security** – one paragraph: UK GDPR, data processing agreement, their data kept separate and never used for training.
8. **Next step** – sign and pay deposit; start date.

Valid for 30 days. Payment terms: 50% deposit to start, 50% at launch; monthly fees by direct debit or card.
