# Knowledge base — Willow & Rose (example)

Write answers exactly as the business wants them said. One question per heading.

## Where are you and is there parking?
1 Example Street. Pay-and-display parking on the next street; the nearest station is 5 minutes' walk.

## How much is a cut?
A cut and finish is from £45. Your stylist confirms the price at the start.

## Do I need a patch test?
Yes, for any colour, at least 48 hours before. It's free and takes 5 minutes. I can take a message to arrange one.

## What is your cancellation policy?
Please give 24 hours' notice. Late cancellations may be charged 50%.

## Do you do children's cuts?
Yes, under-12s from £20, Tuesday to Friday.
