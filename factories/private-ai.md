# Factory: Private AI
Owner: operations-support with the founder · Run: owned by client; maintained by Gabrion or their IT team

1. **Size it** — small office / department / organisation, by users and type of work (fill in the three hardware specs once chosen).
2. **Source it** — established supplier with warranty; deposit from client before ordering.
3. **Image it** — OS, an open-weight model whose licence allows commercial use, private document search over the client's files, simple staff chat interface.
4. **Install it** — on the client's network only, no outside data sharing.
5. **Hand over or manage** — client-managed: docs, admin training, handover checklist. Gabrion-managed: logged VPN used only in agreed maintenance windows; founder approves every remote session.

- [ ] Model licence checked for commercial use and recorded
- [ ] No outbound connections except the agreed update/management link
