# Gabo Sneakers — brand brief (draft v0.1)

- Owner: founder
- Date: 2026-11-20
- Status: concept. No playbook exists for a consumer product brand, so this is "new or unusual" work and every decision below is the founder's.

## The idea in one line
One sneaker for each part of your day (morning training, everyday wear, the commute and the evening), designed as one family so they look like they belong together.

## Why nobody does it this way yet
Sports brands (Nike, ASICS, Salomon) make brilliant performance shoes, but they split them by sport, not by your day. Luxury houses (Chanel, Dior) and design-led labels (Android Homme) make beautiful evening sneakers, but rarely ones you can train or commute in. Gabo sits between the two: **performance comfort with a luxury finish, organised around the day.**

## The line-up

| # | Model (working name) | When | What it must do | Inspiration feel |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Gabo AM** | Morning: gym, then walk | Stable, low-drop forefoot for lifting; cushioned heel for walking; grippy rubber outsole; breathable mesh; quick-lace | Nike training, ASICS Gel, Salomon trail runners |
| 2 | **Gabo Daily** | Everyday | Clean low-top, comfortable cupsole, easy to clean, works with jeans or shorts | Classic everyday court shoe or runner |
| 3 | **Gabo Daily Fine** | A smarter everyday | Same comfort as Daily, but premium full-grain leather or suede, slimmer profile, tonal colours, hidden details | Dior and Chanel quiet-luxury trainers |
| 4 | **Gabo Transit** | Commuting and errands | Durable and weather-resistant, Salomon-style speed-lace, wet-grip outsole, reflective trims; sleek enough for the office; bold colour mixes | Salomon and ASICS city-utility |
| 5 | **Gabo PM** (a range of styles) | Evening and weekend | Statement pieces: comfortable enough for a night out, smart enough for a restaurant | Chanel runners, Dior B-series, Android Homme |

### Gabo Transit: the commute-to-office shoe (founder priority)
The founder is serious about shoes and wants to wear Gabo designs every day, so Transit has to do two jobs: survive the commute and look right in the office.
- **Durable**: abrasion-resistant ripstop or coated-mesh upper, a toe bumper, reinforced heel counter, and a hard-wearing rubber outsole that doesn't wear flat in months.
- **Weather-resistant**: water-resistant upper and gusseted tongue to keep rain and puddles out; deep wet-grip lugs for wet pavements and stations.
- **Salomon-style**: trail-runner shape and speed-lace system, so it's quick on and off.
- **Office-ready**: a toned-down, sleeker version (slimmer sole edge, tonal colours, optional leather or suede overlays) so it doesn't look like a hiking shoe at a desk.
- **Cool colour mixes**: two colourway families. *Commute* has bold technical mixes (e.g. olive/orange, slate/acid green, navy/burnt red). *Office* has tonal mixes (e.g. black/charcoal/bone, stone/tan, grey/cream).

### Gabo PM: evening and weekend styles
- **PM Runner**: luxe runner shape in suede, nylon and leather panels, with metallic or tonal accents.
- **PM High**: statement high-top with a sculpted sole and a zip or strap detail.
- **PM Minimal**: black or bone leather dress sneaker, almost a shoe.
- **PM Weekend**: relaxed suede low-top in seasonal colours.

## What ties the family together
- One signature detail shared by all five (a heel tab, a sole-edge colour or a stitched mark), designed to be Gabo's own.
- One comfort platform (insole and last), tuned per model, so the fit feels the same all day.
- A colour system: AM and Transit in technical tones; Daily in neutrals; Daily Fine and PM in premium tonal colours and black.

## Guard-rails on inspiration
The brands above set the **feel and quality level**, not designs to copy. Gabo's shapes, logos, stripe or panel patterns and soles must be original. Copying recognisable trade dress (a swoosh, ASICS stripes, Dior's Oblique, Chanel's quilting or CC) risks infringement claims and customs seizures.

## Open questions for the founder
1. Who is the first customer: men, women, or unisex sizing?
2. Rough price band per model (AM, Daily and Transit as accessible premium; Daily Fine and PM as premium)?
3. Launch all five, or start with two? Suggestion: **Gabo AM + Gabo Daily Fine**, the clearest contrast and the easiest story.
4. Where to make them: Portugal or Italy for the leather models; Vietnam, China or Indonesia for the performance models?
5. Sell direct online first, or also through a few boutiques?
6. Budget for design and samples before any production order.

## Suggested next steps (founder decides)
1. **Name check**: search "Gabo" on the UK IPO and EUIPO trademark registers (class 25, footwear) and check the domain and social handles before spending on design.
2. **Mood boards**: one per model (can be built in Canva).
3. **Tech packs**: hire a freelance footwear designer to turn this brief into sketches and tech packs.
4. **Factory shortlist**: ask 3–5 factories for minimum order quantities, sample costs and lead times.
5. **Samples**: one sample round of the first two models; wear-test for a month.
6. **Pre-launch**: a landing page with a waiting list to test demand before the first production order.

Any spend, supplier contract, public launch or message to a supplier goes through `memory/approvals/queue.md` first.
